For OwnSearch, you should test all major categories of search queries that people use in everyday life, work, education, and research.
Don't test only simple questions. A proper search engine must handle short keywords, complete questions, technical documentation, current news, financial information, comparisons, troubleshooting, and many other query types.
I've prepared a comprehensive testing checklist with 30 categories and 150 queries. You can run these queries against your existing search engine and evaluate its results.
OwnSearch: Complete Search Engine Testing Checklist
Category 1: General Knowledge
Tests whether OwnSearch can find reliable answers to common questions.
1. Who invented the telephone?
2. What is the largest ocean in the world?
3. Why is the sky blue?
4. What is the difference between weather and climate?
5. How does the internet work?
Check: Are the results directly related to the question, and do they come from trustworthy sources?
